## How it works

The workflow has no local diffusion model. It uses OpenAI nodes from the machine's custom node setup. Your product photo comes in through an image input, and up to eight `LoadImage` nodes can add extra reference images, merged with `ImageBatchMulti`. A `Fast Groups Bypasser (rgthree)` lets you switch groups of nodes on or off.

An `OpenAIChatNode` using `gpt-4.1` looks at the product image. Its `OpenAIChatConfig` instructions (written in Spanish) ask it to design a creative idea and setting for a photo shoot that matches what the product represents, with an example of a detailed perfume bottle prompt. The text it produces becomes the image prompt.

The `OpenAI Image API` node then renders the final picture with `gpt-image-1` at 1024x1536 and high quality.

## Tips

- Use a clear product photo on a simple background so the model recognizes the item.
- Add a prompt of your own when you want a specific setting, or leave it to the chat model to propose one.
- Extra reference images can be added through the optional Load Image groups if you want to guide the style.
- The built-in instructions are in Spanish, but you can edit them in the workflow for another language.
