Our commitment to protecting your data

## Data Privacy

Comfy Deploy is committed to protecting the privacy of our users' data.

Any data you store in your Comfy Deploy account and mark as private will be kept strictly confidential.

We do not share your private data for any purpose other than providing and improving our service.

## Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your private data from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

Only authorized Comfy Deploy personnel have access to systems containing user data, and only for the purpose of maintaining and improving our service.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at privacy@comfydeploy.com.
